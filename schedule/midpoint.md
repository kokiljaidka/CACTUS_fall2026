# Dec 22: Midpoint show-and-tell

**Theme:** Halfway through, everyone shows where their project stands: the question, the data, one result, and one thing the checks caught. No new content and no coding. It is a checkpoint before the January weeks, and a chance to hear what other people are doing.
**Format:** Zoom, one room, about 2 hours. Each person gets 5 minutes to present and 3 minutes of questions. Tuesday, Dec 22. (Dec 29 is a break.)

## What to prepare

Three or four slides (or a one-page document, or a notebook with its output). Keep it to 5 minutes.

| Part | What to show |
|---|---|
| **1. The project** | Your case study and main question, from your project card. Say what changed since Week 1 |
| **2. The data** | Where it came from, how many rows, what one row is, and what you left out. Show your data card if you have one. If you do not have data yet, say what is blocking you and your plan |
| **3. One result** | One chart or number from your own data (Weeks 2 to 5), and **the check that told you it was right** |
| **4. What the log caught** | One thing your verification log caught, or one thing you still do not know |

**Rules**
- Show **public or de-identified data only**. No names, handles or raw posts on screen.
- It is fine if your result is small, or wrong, or not there yet. Showing the check matters more than the result.
- No new reading and no coding is needed.

## What happens on the day

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome, the format, and the order |
| 0:10 | Presentations: 5 minutes each, then 3 minutes of questions (one from a peer, one from a facilitator) |
| about 1:30 | What we heard: the common themes, and what to fix before Week 6 |
| about 1:45 | Look ahead: Weeks 6 to 8 |

Each presenter gets three lines of written feedback afterwards from the facilitators: is the question clear, is the data described honestly, and is there a check?

## Run sheet (Kokil leads)

**Before**
- Ask each person for their order or let them volunteer (a person without data may prefer to go later). Send the order two days before.
- Make a shared sheet for the written feedback (three lines each: question, data, check). Facilitators fill it in during the talks.
- Remind everyone about the data rules (no names or raw posts on screen).
- Have a timekeeper (the Desk): a one-minute warning at 4 minutes, and a stop at 5.

**During:** keep to time (about 9 people times 8 minutes is 72 minutes). Ask one open question after each talk: "what check would you add?" Note anyone with no data yet and who is helping them.

**After:** send each person their feedback within two days. Post the common themes (three to five lines) in the team chat.
