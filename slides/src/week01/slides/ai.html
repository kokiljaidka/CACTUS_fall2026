How we work

AI writes. You decide.

Describe the task

Read what it wrote

Test it

Record the check

This is the skill the whole series builds. You will use AI assistants every week. The skill is not typing code from memory. It is describing a task precisely, reading what the AI wrote, testing it on a small sample, and recording how you checked. Every notebook cell the AI wrote needs a note on how you verified it. You will keep a short prompt-and-verification log.
