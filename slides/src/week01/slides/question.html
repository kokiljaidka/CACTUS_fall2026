Step 2

One main question, two or three sub-questions

Main question

Can we gauge mental health through social media behavior?

Sub-question 1

What are the main reasons people use social media?

Sub-question 2

Which language behaviors predict low mental health?

Sub-question 3

Do motives change the link between use and mental health?

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

The main question is the big one. The sub-questions break it into parts you can actually answer. There is no single right way to break a main question into sub-questions. Notice that each sub-question here points at something you could count, label, or compute. That is what makes it doable.
