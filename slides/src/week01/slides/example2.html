A worked example, step 4

Run the checks on it

Data

4,174 public comments (the Week 2 file). One row is one comment: country, language, site type, date and tone scores.

Measure

Q1: a boycott flag (keywords, on the translated text) and how many comments it catches. Q2: tone, flagged versus not. Q3: that gap by country and site type. Check the flag on 100 hand-labelled comments.

Method

Weeks 2, 3, 5 and 6: clean and group, compare with uncertainty, text and sentiment, check the flag against people.

Ethics

Public comments, with names replaced by a pseudonymous id. We still do not quote or try to identify anyone.

Size

Thailand has 1,595 comments, Hong Kong 13, and splitting makes groups smaller still. Compare with care, or say a group is too small.

Scope

Many languages, and English is machine-translated, so the flag and tone may miss things. Say it as a limit.
