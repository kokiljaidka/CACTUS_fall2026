A worked example, steps 1 to 3

Starbucks comments across Asia

Step 1

A case study

Public online comments that mention Starbucks, from seven places in Southeast and East Asia, 5–14 October 2024.

Topic, place, period. Not “brands on social media”.

Step 2

One main question, sub-questions

How do online comments about Starbucks differ across Southeast and East Asian countries during a boycott period?

How much do comments in each country mention the boycott?

Counting

How does tone differ between countries?

Sentiment

Do the differences hold in every language?

Comparing groups

Step 3

A structure

Comparative. The question is about how places differ, so we compare groups of comments, by country and by language.

Each sub-question says what we would count, score or compare.
