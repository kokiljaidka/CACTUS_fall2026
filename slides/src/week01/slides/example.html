A worked example, steps 1 to 3

Starbucks comments across Asia

Step 1

A case study

Public online comments that mention Starbucks, from seven places in Southeast and East Asia, 5–14 October 2024.

Topic, place, period. Not “brands on social media”.

Step 2

A main question, three sub-questions

Is talking about the boycott linked to how people talk about Starbucks, and does that link depend on where, and on what kind of site, they are posting?

What share of comments mention the boycott, and what else do they talk about (food, prices, politics)?

Describe: counting, topic words

Do comments that mention the boycott use a different tone from those that do not?

Link: sentiment, comparing groups

Does that gap change by country or by kind of site, and how sure are we?

Context: subgroups, uncertainty

Step 3

A structure

Predictors, consequences, moderators. Talking about the boycott is the predictor, tone is the outcome, and country and kind of site are the moderators.

Three levels of depth: describe it, link it, ask when it holds.
