How a week works

Two sessions, plus a little in between

Learning session

About 2 hours. Lecture, worked examples, and a guided activity.

Coding session

About 2 hours. Breakout rooms and a notebook. No lecture.

In between

Required reading, a short practice page, and your verification log.

All sessions are on Zoom. Bring a laptop. No grades, but each week builds on the last.

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

Explain the rhythm. Learning session: Kokil leads and facilitators help anyone who falls behind. Coding session: pairs in breakout rooms, facilitators visit. In between: the textbook sections for the week (required, not graded), a short interactive practice page that ends with a small coding task, and the verification log.
