Counting

Raw counts mislead

Raw top words

the, in, for, of, and ... The little words win in every corpus. They tell you about English, not your topic.

Cleaning steps

Lowercase. Remove stopwords. Drop numbers or links. Each is a choice.

What can go wrong

Lowercasing turns the country "US" into "us". "us" is a stopword. Dropping short words deletes "AI".

Always read the top words after cleaning. Ask what was deleted that you wanted.
