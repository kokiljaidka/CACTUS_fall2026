Dictionary sentiment

Add up the word scores

great = +3   good = +2   bad = -2

"The service was bad" → -2 → negative
"This is not good" → +2 → positive?
    with a rule: after "not", flip the next word → -2 → negative

A dictionary gives each word a score and adds them up. VADER is an English dictionary made for social media, with rules for negation, capitals, and "!". It gives a compound score from -1 to +1.
