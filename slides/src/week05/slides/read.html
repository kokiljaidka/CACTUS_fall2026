Read before you name

Top words are a summary, not the evidence

The method

A clustering method groups texts that use similar words, and prints each group's top words.

The trap

A label is easy to invent from five words. The examples may say something else.

Coherent

Five examples, one topic: you can name it.

Too mixed

General words, unrelated examples: do not name it.

Boilerplate

"Subscribe", "sign in", "newsletter": not content. Remove it.

Read three to five examples from each cluster before you name it.
