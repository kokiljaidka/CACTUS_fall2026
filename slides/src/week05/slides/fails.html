Where it fails

A small gallery

Sarcasm

"Oh great, it deleted my file again." The dictionary sees "great".

A name

"Joy asked about the results." Joy is a person here.

Slang

"That demo was sick!" Positive for a person, negative in the dictionary.

Mixed feelings

"Great team, terrible pay." Plus and minus add up to nothing, and that hides both.

Long texts

Scores tend to go to the extremes in long posts, so averages of long and short texts do not compare well.

What to do

Read a sample by hand. Report how often you and the dictionary disagree.
