Concept

What an estimate claims

A difference in means

The average of one group minus the average of the other.

A regression coefficient

With one yes-or-no predictor, the same difference, plus a standard way to say how uncertain it is.

An interval

A range that shows how much the estimate would move if we ran the study again.

Today's example, made up: a platform adds an “AI-generated” label to some posts. Does it change how likely people say they are to share them?
