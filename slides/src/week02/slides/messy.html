Concept

Messy data is normal

Missing values

Empty cells, “N/A”, “unknown”, “-”.

Duplicates

The same row twice, or the same post collected twice.

Wrong types

Dates and numbers stored as text.

Uneven groups

Some countries have thousands of rows and others a dozen.

Cleaning is not a failure of the data. It is part of the analysis, and each step needs a reason.

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 2

Real data from platforms is messy. Your job is not to make it perfect, it is to know what you changed and why. Every step that removes rows needs a reason you can say out loud. Today's two datasets are both messy on purpose, or by nature.
