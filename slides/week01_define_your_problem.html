Why checking matters

Four ways AI code goes wrong

Silent data loss

The code runs, but rows or values quietly disappear or change.

Invented names

Columns, functions, or packages that do not exist.

Does something else

The code answers a different question from the one you asked.

Confident, wrong explanations

It sounds sure of itself. That is not evidence.

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

How a week works

Two sessions, plus a little in between

Learning session

About 2 hours. Lecture, worked examples, and a guided activity.

Coding session

About 2 hours. Breakout rooms and a notebook. No lecture.

In between

Required reading, a short practice page, and your verification log.

All sessions are on Zoom. Bring a laptop. No grades, but each week builds on the last.

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

Step 1

Pick a case study

Too broad

“Social media”

A case study

How Singapore, Hong Kong, and Taiwan users talk about AI chatbots on Reddit

A case study is a specific topic in a specific context: who it is about, where, and in what period.

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

Step 2

One main question, two or three sub-questions

Main question

Can we gauge mental health through social media behavior?

Sub-question 1

What are the main reasons people use social media?

Sub-question 2

Which language behaviors predict low mental health?

Sub-question 3

Do motives change the link between use and mental health?

CACTUS Fall 2026 · Week 1

Step 3

Pick a structure to start from

StructureBest for
Predictors, consequences, moderatorsA behavior with a clear downstream outcome
Mechanism or mediationHow or why an effect happens
ComparativeTwo platforms, places, groups, or periods
DebateFindings that are visibly mixed
Multi-levelIndividual, platform, and policy angles
Problem, cause, interventionA specific harm and what might fix it

These are starting points, not a checklist. Mix them, or use your own.

Step 4

Is it doable in eight weeks?

Data

Do I have it, or can I get it by Week 4? What is one row?

Size

Big enough to say something, small enough for a laptop?

Measure

For each sub-question, what would I count, label, or compute?

Method

Which week of this series teaches the tool I need?

Ethics

Public data or consent? Could anyone be identified?

Scope

Could I answer it in a few weeks of part-time coding?

Too big: How does social media affect society? Better: How do Singapore and Taiwan Reddit users talk about AI-generated art?
