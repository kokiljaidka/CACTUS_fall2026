# Before you collect: questions to ask

This is a list of questions, not a set of rules. It does not tell you what is allowed. The answers depend on the platform, your project, and your university. Answer each question for your own data, write the answers in your data card, and **ask when you are not sure**.

## 1. What am I collecting, and from whom?

- Whose words or behavior are in this data?
- Did they post in a public space, or in a space with limited membership (a private group, a closed forum)?
- Could they reasonably expect researchers to read and keep it?

## 2. What do the terms say?

- Where did I read the platform's terms of use, and when?
- Do they allow collection for research? Do they allow me to store the data? Do they allow me to share it, and with whom?
- Does the platform ask me to delete content if the author deletes it?
- If I use a route the platform does not offer (such as scraping a website), do its terms or its robots rules forbid it?

## 3. Does my ethics process apply?

- Does my university require approval, or an exemption, for collecting this kind of data?
- Who do I ask? (Write the name or office here.)
- Have I asked before collecting, not after?

## 4. How sensitive is it?

- Does it involve health, sexuality, religion, politics, immigration status, finances, minors, or anything else that could harm someone if linked to them?
- Is anyone in the data vulnerable?
- Do I need this data to answer my question, or only some of it?

## 5. What do I keep, and what do I protect?

- Which fields does my question need? Which can I drop?
- How were the posts chosen: all of them, a random sample, the platform's top-ranked ones, or a keyword search? That decides who and what is missing.
- What identifies a person (names, handles, links, exact times, small places, distinctive text)? How will I remove or change it?
- Am I guessing anything about individual people (gender, religion, caste, nationality) from their names, handles, or pictures? If so, why, and should I?
- Even after that, could someone be found? What could they search?
- Where will I store the raw data, who holds the original files, and who can see them?
- Am I allowed to send these texts to an AI service (a chat tool or an API)? De-identify them first (names, handles, links, anything that points to a person), send only what I need, and check the platform's terms, the data owner's terms, and my ethics process.
- Where is my API key, and is it out of every file I share?

## 6. What will I share, and how?

- Will I share the data, or only results? With whom?
- If I quote, could a quote be searched and traced back to a person?
- What do I do if someone asks me to remove their post?

## 7. What do I record?

- Have I written a data card: what, when, how, which terms, which ethics process, what I removed, and who is missing?
- Could someone else repeat my collection from my record?

## Who to ask

- Your supervisor.
- The CACTUS ethics contact: **Kokil Jaidka**. Ask before you collect, in the group channel or in a session.
- The NUS ethics review process (the Institutional Review Board), if your project needs formal approval.
- The platform's developer terms or support pages.

When in doubt, **ask before you collect.**
