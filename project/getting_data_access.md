# Getting data access

In Week 4 you work on data for your own project. Most of you will bring **an export you already have**. **If you do not have data yet, talk to the facilitators offline in Week 1, and have your data by Week 3.** The facilitators can help with Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, and Bluesky data.

Some platforms release data in minutes, some take days, and some change their rules without notice. Plan for a delay.

This is a shared page: **when you find out what works, tell us** so the next person does not have to repeat it.

## What to do, in order

1. **Choose a source** that can answer your project's main question. If you are unsure, ask the facilitators. Your project card says what one row of your data is.
2. **Check whether you already have it**: an export, a dataset from your lab, data from an earlier project.
3. **If not, talk to the facilitators in Week 1.** Say which platform you need. They will tell you the usual way to get it.
4. **Answer the questions below** for your source. Official documentation beats a blog post from last year.
5. **If you use a key or login, store it safely.** In Colab Secrets. Never in a notebook, a chat with an AI, a repository, or a message.
6. **Look at what you got**: what the columns are, how many items there are, and whose data is in it.
7. **Write down what you did.** Add it to the table below.

If you still have no data by the Week 4 coding session, you can use the saved example. You will not be left without something to work on, but you need your own data before Week 6.

## The questions to answer for any platform

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What kind of account do I need (personal, developer, institutional)? | Some need a developer account or a stated research purpose |
| Is approval needed, and how long does it take? | Plan for the delay |
| Where do I get the data, and do I need a key or login? | If you need a key, it goes in Colab Secrets |
| What are the limits (items per export, requests per day, how far back)? | Decides how much you can get |
| Do the terms allow collection for research, and for storing and sharing data? | Decides what you may keep and what you may share |
| Is there a cost? | Some access is paid |
| Does my ethics process need to approve this data? | Check before you collect, not after. See the [Ethics checklist](ethics_checklist.md) |

## Help

- **Talk to the facilitators offline in Week 1** if you do not have data. They can help with Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, and Bluesky data.
- Ask in the group chat. Someone has probably done it.
- Pair up with a student on the same platform.
- **Access clinic** (optional, 30 minutes, in Week 3): bring your question and a facilitator who knows that platform will help.
- If a rule is unclear, or the data may be sensitive, ask before you collect.
