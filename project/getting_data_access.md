# Getting data access

In Week 4 you collect data from a platform for your own project. **Start getting access now.** Some platforms approve access in minutes, some take days, and some change their rules without notice. Plan for a delay.

You are graduate students, so you are expected to work this out yourselves. This page helps you do that, and it is a shared page: **when you find out what works, tell us** so the next person does not have to repeat it.

## What to do, in order

1. **Choose a source** that can answer your project's main question. If you are unsure, ask in the group chat or at the access clinic. Your project card says what one row of your data is.
2. **Find out what access needs.** For your platform, answer the questions below. Official documentation beats a blog post from last year.
3. **Apply or sign up.** Do it this week.
4. **Store your key safely.** In Colab Secrets. Never in a notebook, a chat with an AI, a repository, or a message.
5. **Make one small test request** (a handful of items) and look at the raw response.
6. **Write down what you did.** Add it to the table below.

If you are still waiting for access by the Week 4 coding session, you can use a small saved sample or an export. You will not be left without something to work on.

## The questions to answer for any platform

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What kind of account do I need (personal, developer, institutional)? | Some need a developer account or a stated research purpose |
| Is approval needed, and how long does it take? | Plan for the delay |
| Where do I get the key or credentials, and what are they called? | You will paste a key into Colab Secrets |
| What are the limits (requests per day or per minute, items per page, how far back)? | Decides how much you can collect and how you must pause |
| Do the terms allow collection for research, and for storing and sharing data? | Decides what you may keep and what you may share |
| Is there a cost? | Some access is paid |
| Does my ethics process need to approve this data? | Check before you collect, not after |

## Help

- Ask in the group chat. Someone has probably done it.
- Pair up with a student on the same platform.
- **Access clinic** (optional, 30 minutes, in Week 3): bring your question and a facilitator who has tried that platform will help.
- If a rule is unclear, or the data may be sensitive, ask before you collect.
