# Who is in CACTUS this fall

Everyone in this series brings their own research project. This page is where we introduce ourselves to each other: what each of us is working on, and what we hope to learn. It is a way to find people to ask, and to see how many different questions the same methods can answer.

**It is your choice what appears here.** Nothing about you is shown unless you tell us. You can change or remove your profile at any time, just tell the facilitators.

## How to be profiled

Pick one:

| Option | What is shown |
|---|---|
| **Just the project** | Your first name (or a name you choose) and your project in one line |
| **A short profile** | The above, plus what you hope to learn and a link (a website, a profile, or a repository) if you want |
| **Not on the page** | Nothing. You are still part of CACTUS |
